M4FIA

M4FIA takes place in a modern town whose governemtn is on the brink of collapse. The people have fled and only the officials, enforcers, and 'special' individuals remain, each driven by their own motives, bound together by distrust and desperation.

REMNANTS

The last loyalists of a dying government. Though the system is flawed and riddled with corruption, they believe it can still be saved. By uncovering the truth, rooting out conspirators, and restoring trust, they seek to rebuild order before the town falls into complete ruin.

SYNDICATE

A hidden network of corrupt officials, profiteers, and power-hungry opportunists. They believe the current system is beyond saving and intend to accelerate its collapse. If they succeed, they intend to forge a new order built to serve their interests alone.

NULL

Neither loyalists nor conspirators. These individuals answer only to themselves. Some seek revenge for old wounds, others thrive in chaos, and some simply wish to entertain themselves in the drama.

What is M4FIA?

✦ M4FIA is an expansion or a new take of the original Mafia something something

What is this website for?

✦ This website contains all the relevant information and resources for the custom-made Mafia game “M4FIA” created by @CliffordLuke. You can view the lore, rules, and role information of the game in the Wiki, and even suggest new features and/or roles in the Suggestions Page.

How can you play?

✦ Refer to the M4FIA Wiki in order to learn about the different components of the game, including the different lore, rules, and roles in the game. You may also use the Game Tracker to help you run the game for your friends.

How many players can play?

✦ Around 4+ players can play, but the recommended player count is 6 or more, excluding the Host, who is in charge of running the game. The Host may use the Game Tracker in order to make managing the game a lighter task.

Can I make suggestions?

✦ Yes. You can head to the Suggestions page to anonymously (or ononymously) suggest new features to the game/website, or even new roles.
